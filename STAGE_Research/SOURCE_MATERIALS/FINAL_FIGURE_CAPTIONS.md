# Figure captions (F01-F16; each states dataset, years, and boundary)

F01. Annual road accidents in India, 2018-2024 (verified MoRTH benchmark). Recorded totals
fluctuated with a 2020 decline; counts are not exposure-normalized risk.
F02. Annual road fatalities in India, 2018-2024 (MoRTH benchmark). Fatalities rose 12.4%, faster
than accidents; fitted slopes are descriptive (N=7, ns).
F03. Fatality ratio, deaths per 100 accidents, 2018-2024 (derived). Rose 33.50 to 36.33; a
case-fatality-style measure, not a population rate.
F04. Zone accident trends by six zones (benchmark + mapping). Descriptive differences only; no ranking.
F05. State-year accident heatmap, 38 entities x 2018-2024. Missing (NV) shown as missing, never zero.
F06. State accident/fatality slopes with 95% CI (OLS, N=7). Wide intervals; most non-detectable.
F07. State-year rainfall vs accidents (IMD join, N=236). Pooled r=-0.234; scale confounding likely; not causal.
F08. State-year mean Tmax vs fatalities (N=236). Pooled r=+0.498; associational only.
F09. 2024 fatalities by reported violation category (MoRTH AR2024 mirror, India-level). Over-speeding
70.3% is a reporting share, not proven causation.
F10. 2024 accidents by collision type (AR2024 mirror). Descriptive configuration mix.
F11. 2024 fatalities by road-user mode (AR2024 mirror). Counts without exposure; largest count is not highest risk.
F12. 2024 licence-status accidents and safety-device non-use toll (AR2024 mirror). Reported categories only.
F13. 2024 monthly road accidents, NCRB ADSI Table 1A.5 Road-States (NCRB vintage, NOT MoRTH). May peak, July trough.
F14. Tamil Nadu district accidents 2021-2023 (district CSV; sums == benchmark TN). TN-only evidence.
F15. Actual vs predicted accidents with test-RMSE comparison (2023-2024 holdout). Naive persistence
outperformed all trained models; honest null result.
F16. K-means K-selection and PCA cluster scatter (K=2, silhouette 0.356). Exploratory typology; descriptive labels.
