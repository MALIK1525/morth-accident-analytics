# Conclusion (answers RQ1-RQ11 from evidence only)

Recorded accidents fluctuated (+3.7%, 2020 dip) while fatalities rose faster (+12.4%) across
2018-2024, lifting severity to 36.3/100 accidents (RQ1). States/zones differ descriptively; most
state trends lack statistical detectability at N=7 (RQ2). Annual series fluctuate; 2024 NCRB monthly
peaks in May and troughs in July under a separate vintage (RQ3). Rainfall correlates negatively and
temperature positively with counts at pooled level; severity shows no association; nothing causal (RQ4).
Over-speeding dominates reported violations (70.3% of fatalities); Hit from Back leads collision counts
(RQ5-6). Two-wheelers are 46.2% of deaths by count, not risk (RQ7). Licence/safety data describe
reported categories only (RQ8). No trained model beat naive persistence on 2023-2024 holds (RQ9).
States split into two descriptive profiles (RQ10). Vehicle exposure, microdata, and monthly MoRTH
series remain unavailable, blocking risk and causal inference (RQ11).
