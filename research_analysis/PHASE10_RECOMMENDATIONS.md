# Recommendations (evidence-linked; each needs further validation)

## A. Data-system
1. Publish state-level registered-vehicle STOCK annually in machine-readable form (evidence: EX-01
   India-only blocks all per-vehicle rates; limitation: definitional work needed on dead stock).
2. Release MoRTH monthly state series (evidence: seasonality only possible under NCRB vintage).
3. Open eDAR aggregates with coordinates at district level (evidence: hotspot analysis blocked).

## B. Research
4. Extend panel years before ML retraining (evidence: N=213, persistence dominates).
5. Within-state designs with longer series (evidence: pooled scale confounding).
6. Extract NCRB time-of-day/state tables to CSV (evidence: Table 1A.6 discovered PDF-only).

## C. Analytical (road-safety)
7. Track severity (deaths/100 acc), not just counts (evidence: 33.5 -> 36.3 rise).
8. Treat violation shares as reporting signals, not proven causes (evidence: CS-01 definitions).

## D. Technology/app
9. Keep benchmark/uploaded-data mode separation (evidence: Phase 2 workspace design).
10. Surface vintage labels and N/A states in every dashboard module (evidence: audit findings).
