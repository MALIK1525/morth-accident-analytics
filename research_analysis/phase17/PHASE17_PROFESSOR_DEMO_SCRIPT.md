   MoRTH 2018–2024 research benchmark is separate and untouched."
2. (30s) Hover Maharashtra: tooltip shows 2024 reported accidents 36,118,
   fatalities 15,715 with year + MoRTH label — historical, not live.
3. (60s) Click Tamil Nadu: panel shows accidents/fatalities/injured per year
   (injured 2023–24 reads 'Not available for this year' — never zero),
   severity with formula, year-trend table, centroid weather labeled as proxy.
4. (45s) Weather: "10 monitored cities, Open-Meteo source, timestamps. If the
   provider is unreachable you see a STALE badge with the last good time —
   never presented as current."
5. (30s) Weather Near Me: click only on request; denial breaks nothing;
   coordinates never leave the browser session.
6. (45s) Data Watch defaults to accident-relevant sets; NCRB carries a
   never-merge warning; discovery never enters the benchmark.
7. (30s) Limitations: no public live accident feed; city-proxy weather;
   TomTom key absent (awaiting-state by design); NCRB blocked; watch history
   ephemeral. "Every honest null on this page is intentional."

Do NOT claim: live accidents, causal weather, safest/dangerous states,
prediction, or India-wide weather from 10 cities.
