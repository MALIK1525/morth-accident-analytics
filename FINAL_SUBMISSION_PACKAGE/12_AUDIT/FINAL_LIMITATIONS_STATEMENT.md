# Limitations statement (academic)
Recorded accident counts should not be interpreted as exposure-normalized risk in the absence of a
reliable state-wise traffic exposure denominator. Also: no vehicle stock/AADT/vehicle-km; no
microdata/coords (eDAR restricted); no driver/vehicle detail; no humidity/visibility/fog/wind; no
MoRTH monthly series; single-state districts; state injuries end 2022; conditional 2014-17 history
(J&K break, DNH merge); ML panel N=213 with persistence dominance; projection (not census) denominators.
