# Conclusion statement
Studied: geography/time/weather/reported-cause/collision/road-user/licence/safety patterns in Indian
road accidents 2018-2024 (+ conditional history, NCRB monthly, IMD weather). Found: fluctuating counts
with faster-rising deaths; pooled weather associations; null severity-weather and null ML-vs-baseline
results; two descriptive state profiles. Statistically supported: 6 weather associations (FDR),
zone differences (Friedman), monthly non-uniformity. Not supported: causal, risk-ranking, forecasting,
and per-vehicle claims. Unavailable: vehicle stock, microdata, monthly MoRTH, several environments.
Framework enables future uploads, integrations, and re-analysis under the same provenance discipline.
